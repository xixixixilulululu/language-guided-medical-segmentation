# Phase 1 — Post-hoc sensitivity check: LViT-T on MosMedData+ with batch size 24

Report of the check planned in [PHASE1_SENSITIVITY_LVIT_PLAN.md](PHASE1_SENSITIVITY_LVIT_PLAN.md) (commit
52ad71b, pushed before any run of this check was submitted), reported as fixed there.

- **This check was decided after the Phase 1 main results had been seen.** It is a post-hoc sensitivity
  analysis.
- **The Phase 1 main results and conclusions are unchanged.** [PHASE1_RESULTS.md](PHASE1_RESULTS.md) and
  [PHASE1_SUMMARY.md](PHASE1_SUMMARY.md) keep the main-run LViT-T value (batch size 2, Dice 61.26).
- **These runs are not part of the six-method analysis.** They do not enter `src/analyze_phase1.py` or its
  statistics.

## 1. What was run

- LViT-T on MosMedData+, seeds 1001 / 1002 / 1003.
- **The only change from the main runs is batch size 24** (the LViT paper's default, Sec. IV-C) instead of 2:
  `configs/lvit_sensitivity_b24.yaml`.
- Everything else is the same:
  - the frozen main-run driver `src/phase1/train_lvit.py`, unchanged;
  - learning rate 1e-3 and the schedule;
  - early stopping with patience 50;
  - checkpoint selection on validation Dice only;
  - the data, the split and the common evaluator (probability > 0.5).
- Before training, each job verified:
  - the frozen LViT-T record;
  - the pinned LViT commit;
  - the sha256 of the sensitivity configuration.

## 3. Description (no significance test, as planned)

- **Dice.** With batch size 24 and every other setting unchanged, the mean test Dice of LViT-T on
  MosMedData+ is 74.01 ± 1.11. That is 12.75 points above the main runs (61.26 ± 1.10) and 0.56 points below
  the paper (74.57). Every batch-24 seed (72.82–75.03) is above every batch-2 seed (60.13–62.33).
- **Empty predictions.** There are 0–2 per seed with batch 24, against 38–41 with batch 2.
- **Boundary metrics.** The mean HD95 is 18.19 px with batch 24 and 62.04 px with batch 2; the mean ASSD is
  5.53 px and 50.29 px.
- **Training.** The batch-24 runs stopped later (epochs 188–298, against 88–208).
- **What this shows.** The batch size is the only setting that differs between the two sets of runs. With
  three seeds per setting and no test, this report states what changed, not why.
