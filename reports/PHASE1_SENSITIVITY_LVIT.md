## 3. Description (no significance test, as planned)

- **Dice.** With batch size 24 and every other setting unchanged, the mean test Dice of LViT-T on
  MosMedData+ is 74.01 ± 1.11. That is 12.75 points above the main runs (61.26 ± 1.10) and 0.56 points below
  the paper (74.57). Every batch-24 seed (72.82–75.03) is above every batch-2 seed (60.13–62.33).
- **Empty predictions.** There are 0–2 per seed with batch 24, against 38–41 with batch 2.
- **Boundary metrics.** The mean HD95 is 18.19 px with batch 24 and 62.04 px with batch 2; the mean ASSD is
  5.53 px and 50.29 px.
- **Training.** The batch-24 runs stopped later (epochs 188–298, against 88–208).
- **What this shows.** The batch size is the only setting that differs between the two sets of runs. With
  three seeds per setting and no test, this report states what changed, not why.

## 5. Post-hoc supplementary analysis: MosMedData+ six-method comparison with LViT-T batch 24

> **POST-HOC SUPPLEMENTARY ANALYSIS.** It was decided after the Phase 1 main results and the sensitivity
> results above had been seen. **It does not replace the Phase 1 main results:** the main six-method table
> and the 15 pairwise comparisons in [PHASE1_RESULTS.md](PHASE1_RESULTS.md) stay as they are.

### 5.1 Method (written and pushed before the computation)

- **Scope.** MosMedData+ only; QaTa-COV19 is not affected.
- **Data.** The three LViT-T batch-24 runs (`runs/lvit_b24/MosMedData+/seed<k>/eval_test/per_case.csv`)
  replace the three main LViT-T runs. The other five methods (U-Net, TransUNet, nnU-Net, RecLMIS, DD-CMD)
  use their main-run results unchanged.
- **Statistics: exactly those of the fixed Phase 1 analysis.** `src/analyze_phase1.py` is not modified;
  `src/phase1/posthoc_mosmed_lvit_b24.py` imports it and calls its own functions (`load_per_case`,
  `main_table`, `pairwise_table`). These are:
  - six-method table: mean ± sample SD of the 3 per-seed means; Dice 95% CI = case bootstrap of the
    seed-averaged per-case Dice (10,000 resamples, rng 0);
  - 15 pairwise comparisons: paired t-test on the seed-averaged per-case Dice, Holm across the 15 pairs,
    two-sided α = 0.05; the Wilcoxon signed-rank test with its own Holm correction as a supplement;
  - mIoU / HD95 / ASSD: paired mean differences with t-CI and case-bootstrap CI.
- **Check of the script.** Run without the substitution (`--no-substitute`), it reproduces the MosMedData+
  rows of the main analysis (`results/final_six_method_results.csv`, `results/final_six_method_pairwise.csv`)
  exactly. This was checked before the computation.
- **Outputs.** `results/phase1_posthoc_mosmed_lvit_b24_results.csv` and
  `results/phase1_posthoc_mosmed_lvit_b24_pairwise.csv`.
- **Caveats.** The analysis was decided after the results were seen. The 15 tests are a new family, so its
  p-values are not those of the pre-specified analysis. The MosMedData+ slices are correlated, as noted for
  the main analysis.

### 5.3 Description (post-hoc)

- **Primary test.** With the batch-24 LViT-T, 9 of the 15 pairs are significant (main analysis: 12 of 15).
  - The four methods U-Net, LViT-T, RecLMIS and DD-CMD (Dice 74.01–74.85) do not differ significantly from
    each other: all 6 pairs among them have Holm p ≥ 0.249.
  - Each of these four is significantly better than nnU-Net and TransUNet, and nnU-Net is significantly
    better than TransUNet.
  - LViT-T vs. DD-CMD has raw p = 0.042, and its unadjusted 95% CI just excludes 0 ([−1.65, −0.03]), but the
    pair is not significant after Holm (p = 0.249).
- **Wilcoxon supplement.** It disagrees with the primary test on 5 pairs:
  - U-Net vs. nnU-Net, nnU-Net vs. LViT-T and nnU-Net vs. RecLMIS are significant only by the t-test;
  - LViT-T vs. DD-CMD and RecLMIS vs. DD-CMD are significant only by the Wilcoxon test.
- **Boundary metrics.** The batch-24 LViT-T has a lower HD95 than U-Net (−4.84 px [−7.47, −2.20]) and a
  higher HD95 than RecLMIS (+2.17 px), whose t-CI [−0.03, +4.38] includes 0.
- **Status.** All of this is post hoc. It does not change the pre-specified Phase 1 comparison, in which
  LViT-T (batch 2) is significantly below the other five methods on MosMedData+.
